- **Asset exclusion now beats the Fed path.** Spec task 3 says "EVERY path", so "Gold miners rally as Fed signals rate cut" (kept since B6, pinned at `tests/test_event_context.py:265`) becomes excluded, and "Yen falls as US yields rise" style pins flip. "Wall Street" counts as a US marker only when it does not lead the title.
- **"Dollar" counts as a soft US marker.** It is not in the spec's marker list, but task 3 requires "Dollar rises after hot CPI" to stay, and without it the same headline shape dies in the jobs, PPI, GDP and retail families. FOMC and CPI keep their tuned `off_subject` rule as their US-subject rule; every other family, including `other` (Core PCE, UoM), gets `no_us_subject`.
- **No company list.** A foreign company without a US marker fails the marker test, so Westpac needs no list. A US company without a marker ("Disney plans several thousand layoffs") also goes: the rule asks for a title marker, as ruled. A title with a hard marker passes even when a foreign name leads ("Canada slaps tariffs on US goods"), as the spec's "without a US marker" says. The replay will show the residue.
- **Own-asset exemption lives at rule level only.** I add no keyword lists for Crude Oil Inventories or Natural Gas Storage: that would switch on scoring for titles that have none, a separate decision under the causation-matrix-first rule.
- **Whole-word matching tolerates a plural or possessive** (`(?:'?s)?`), otherwise "rate hike" would miss "rate hikes" and "ISM" would miss "ISM's".
- **Evidence count.** n is taken from the final pre-release XAUUSD accumulator row: evidence rows when that row has them; for rows before 2026-10-22 the non-zero entries of `top_contributions_json`, which is a lower bound capped at 3 (enough for a floor of 3); `None` when neither exists, which is a no-call "too few articles (n unknown)". The floor can only fail closed on old rows. The same n gates a print-call lean, because print rows hold no sentiment.
- **The floor changes the call, not the Combined number.** A below-floor lean still enters the 70/30 blend exactly as a sub-0.2 lean does today ("70/30 unchanged"). Combined gets no new state.
- **Extra hard-rule guard `summary_only`.** The spec's guards read headlines, and id 7's headline trips none of them. A phrase found only in the summary goes to the classifier. Without this, id 7 cannot be a regression test.
- **`us_link` unknown leaves the rule tier in triage**, so 12 of the 23 hard-rule phrases (war, tanker and pipeline attacks, default, downgrade, tariffs, emergency and unscheduled rate moves) now wait for the classifier. That is what spec 4 says; it also means every past rule alert changes route.
- **Poller for Existing Home Sales** (`fred` source, no twin) with a gate tolerance of 2% on `previous`, because ALFRED has no vintages and NAR revises monthly. The tolerance checks series and units, not first prints. Rows older than the latest stay empty through the existing `later_release` rule; the poller also stops 3 days after release; the one-off backlog may fill the latest row after that window because no later release exists (spec 6g).
- **Census advance titles use the Census API as the poller source and the FRED mirror as twin and first-print source.** Both are in the registry, so a gate failure leaves them amber per R3. Their older rows fill through the B8 ALFRED pass, which needs a small fix for one-point vintages.
