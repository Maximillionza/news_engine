## What the sweep found that changes priorities (verified)

  - misses Core CPI m/m and Final GDP;

- **The nowcast-capture task is stuck.** [Certain] The 10-19 run of `tier1-nowcast-release-eve` has been "running" for over a day. There were no runs on 10-18 or 10-20, and 0 live snapshots exist. Claude scheduled tasks are an unreliable host for jobs that need no LLM. The Windows tasks all run cleanly.
- **Your dipstick question, answered by the data.** [Certain] The Cleveland CPI nowcast for the month being released stops changing at the end of that month, because CPI released mid-October measures September prices. Comparing the value 5 days before release with the final value gives identical numbers in 17 of 17 releases. An oil move in the days before CPI changes next month's CPI, not this one, so a 12-hours-before capture adds nothing for CPI. The useful dipstick is mid-reference-month (about 25 days before release) against the final value, and that can be run retroactively.
- **The shock classifier is running on Ollama alone.** [Certain] The Anthropic credits are exhausted (155 errors). Every alert since 10-15 was classified by Ollama; the 42 rule-default fallbacks predate the auto-start fix. Each cycle still tries Claude first and waits out a cooldown.
- **Non-US events alert as High with buy/sell leans.** [Certain] Examples: Yandex data centre (also 465 minutes stale), Russian drone base, Houthi camp. 6 of the 7 High alerts with price data saw no real move. Shock-lean accuracy so far is 14 of 26 at 5 minutes, a coin flip. Medium alerts (56 of 75) are never graded.
- **The article read takes in wrong-sign and off-topic stories.** [Certain] Generic FOMC keywords ("central bank", "rate cut") admit foreign central banks. An ECB Schnabel story entered the 10-14 CPI rows at +0.75 USD (inverted), and 92 of 137 FOMC-family top contributions have no Fed term at all. FinBERT maps tone to USD with no notion of subject.
- **Stats overstates skill.** [Certain/Likely] "In line" counts as a call. A constant "in line" answer would score 59% against Tier 1's 65% on the CPI family. The CPI m/m news scale of 0.0 makes 23 of 31 News calls "in line", and switching to sigma alone won't fix it.
- **Same-incident repeats are re-alerted.** [Certain] Identical URLs were pushed as new alerts: Riyadh blast twice in 54 minutes, both High; Hormuz 3 hours apart. A shared URL alone is unsafe, though: CNBC's daily oil live-blog URL carried opposite stories.
- **Waste and staleness.** [Certain] The outcome confirmer re-fetches the same 28 below-threshold predictions every 15 minutes, and its log is 3.5 MB. `macro_calendar` has nothing after 2026-09-17. Five of the node test failures trace to jsdom missing from the live worktree; the fifth "failure" is a helper file.
- **Security.** [Certain] The Alpha Vantage key at `HANDOVER.md:52` is in 5 remote refs, including origin/master, and in the News_Engine2 repo.

## Owner decisions already made (2026-10-20)
- Same-incident follow-ups: **link and label** ("Update to incident #N"). No new push or lean unless severity rises or polarity flips.
- Stale alerts: **send, tagged with age.** Bands: Fresh ≤5 min, Delayed 5–15, Stale 15–60, Very stale >60. Age = alert time − earliest article publish time, with URL memory across alerts.
- Pre-release Tier 1 refresh: **morning of release**. A weekly run plus a daily 07:00 SAST run, both catching up on wake. No overnight or 12h job, which the nowcast evidence makes unnecessary for CPI.
- Stats Combined: **informational agree/disagree marker**; 70/30 unchanged; never a "conflicting" status.
- Earlier rulings stand: country/US-linkage is built; the Market reaction tab stays separate from shock alerts; mover/anchor is a once-off study first; the article read is strengthened, not discounted; **all ACE/EA/MT5 material is removed**.
