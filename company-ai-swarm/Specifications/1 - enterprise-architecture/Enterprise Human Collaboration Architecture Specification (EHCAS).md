# 1. Purpose

The Enterprise Human Collaboration Architecture Specification defines how humans and AI agents collaborate within The Company.

EHCAS governs:

- Human authority.

- AI authority.

- Executive interaction.

- Human escalation.

- Approval models.

- Collaboration interfaces.

- Organizational adoption.


# 2. Definition

The Human-AI Collaboration Model defines the relationship between:

- Human leadership.

- AI leadership.

- AI agents.

- Enterprise systems.

- External stakeholders.

The Company is not a fully autonomous replacement system.

It is a governed intelligence organization.


# 3. Collaboration Principles

## Principle 1 — Humans Define Purpose

Humans establish:

- Mission.

- Values.

- Strategic objectives.

- Acceptable boundaries.


## Principle 2 — AI Provides Execution Intelligence

AI provides:

- Analysis.

- Planning.

- Execution.

- Optimization.

- Automation.


## Principle 3 — Authority Must Be Explicit

No agent assumes authority.

Authority is granted through governance (see AOSS §9 Authority Model, §28 Autonomy Model).


## Principle 4 — Escalation Is a Feature

Uncertainty should trigger collaboration, not uncontrolled action.


## Principle 5 — Accountability Remains Traceable

Every outcome has an accountable authority.

# 5. Human Roles Within The Company

Humans SHALL operate in roles including:


# 5.1 Strategic Authority

Responsibilities:

- Define objectives.

- Approve major decisions.

- Establish values.

- Set risk tolerance.


# 5.2 Domain Experts

Responsibilities:

- Provide specialist judgement.

- Validate outputs.

- Review complex cases.


# 5.3 Governance Authorities

Responsibilities:

- Approve policies.

- Manage compliance.

- Review risk.


# 5.4 System Owners

Responsibilities:

- Maintain capabilities.

- Approve changes.

- Manage operational health.

## Executive Intelligence

Provides:

- Strategic analysis.

- Recommendations.


## Operational Intelligence

Provides:

- Coordination.

## Specialist Agents

# 7. Human Approval Requirements

Human approval SHALL be required for:

- Major strategic changes.

- Legal commitments.

- High-risk actions.

- Irreversible decisions.

- External commitments.


# 8. Human Escalation Model

Agents SHALL escalate when:

- Confidence is low.

- Conflicting objectives exist.

- Policy interpretation is unclear.

- Risk exceeds threshold.

- Human judgement is required.


Example:

```
Agent: "Two valid legal interpretations exist."
Action: Escalate to Legal Authority.
```


# 9. Collaboration Interface

Humans SHALL interact through:

- Executive dashboards.

- Task interfaces.

- Decision reviews.

- Approval workflows.

- Knowledge contributions.


# 10. Human Feedback Integration

Human feedback MAY update:

- Agent behaviour.

- Workflow design.

- Knowledge.

- Policies.


Feedback SHALL be validated before becoming enterprise learning.


# 11. Human Override Capability

Authorized humans SHALL be able to:

- Pause workflows.

- Reject decisions.

- Modify objectives.

- Restrict agents.


Overrides SHALL be recorded.


# 12. Decision Authority Class

Decisions SHALL be classified:

## Autonomous

Low-risk repeatable decisions.


## Assisted

AI recommends, human confirms.


## Collaborative

AI and humans jointly evaluate.


## Human Authority

Human makes final decision.


This classification answers "who decides this class of decision" and is evaluated per
decision type; it is independent of AOSS's Authority Level (an agent's general organizational
rank) and AOSS's Autonomy Level (an agent's assigned execution independence).

# 14. Human Trust Model

Trust SHALL be built through:

Transparency.

Consistency.

Performance.

Explainability.

Control.


# 15. AI Transparency Requirements

For significant actions, AI SHALL provide:

- Objective.

- Reasoning summary.

- Evidence.

- Alternatives considered.

- Confidence.

- Risks.


# 16. Human Capability Development

The Company SHALL support humans in developing:

- AI collaboration skills.

- Decision oversight.

- Domain expertise.

- Governance capability.


# 17. Human Knowledge Integration

Human expertise SHALL enter The Company through:

- Validated knowledge.

- Policies.

- Reviews.

- Domain contributions.


# 18. Collaboration Metrics

The Company SHALL measure:

- Human satisfaction.

- Escalation quality.

- Decision improvement.

- Productivity improvement.

- Trust levels.


# 19. Human-AI Conflict Resolution

When humans and AI disagree:

The Company SHALL:

1. Identify disagreement.

2. Review evidence.

3. Evaluate assumptions.

4. Apply the Decision Authority Class (§12) and AOSS Authority Model.

5. Record outcome.


# 20. Human Collaboration Security

Human interactions SHALL enforce:

- Identity verification.

- Authorization.

- Auditability.

- Data protection.


# 21. Human Collaboration Invariants

The following SHALL always be true:

- Humans define purpose.

- AI operates within authority.

- Humans retain accountability.

- Agents cannot bypass governance.

- Escalation is available.

- Decisions remain traceable.

- Human expertise remains valuable.

- AI augments organisational capability.


# 22. Design Philosophy

The Company is not designed as an artificial replacement for an organization.

It is designed as an organization where intelligence is distributed.

Humans provide:

Purpose.

Judgement.

Values.

Accountability.

AI provides:

Scale.

Speed.

Analysis.

Execution.

Together they form a new enterprise model:

A human-guided, AI-operated organization.
