## Raw idea as presented (verbatim from user)
Customers in South Africa seeking home improvement and trade services (bricklayers, electricians, plumbers, architects, engineers) struggle to locate vendors they can trust to deliver on quality, cost, and timeline. Current practice relies on informal referral networks with no standardized credibility signal and no financial protection if work is unsatisfactory.

Vendors, in turn:

Carry payment risk (clients withholding payment despite contracted work being delivered)
Have no portable, cross-platform credibility record
Have no protection against being "held hostage" by difficult clients post-delivery

Who has this problem: Individual/household customers commissioning single-trade jobs, and independent or small-team artisans seeking client flow — specifically in the South African informal-to-semi-formal trades market.

Evidence source: Small sample of friends/colleagues reporting difficulty locating reliable vendors. [Anecdotal — not market-validated]
