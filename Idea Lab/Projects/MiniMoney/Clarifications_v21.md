## Required change 4 — curriculum authorship: RESOLVED via MVP scope
## decision (supersedes the original framing of this question)
**Curriculum/educational content is not part of the MVP or launch scope.**
The user's verbatim framing: "This is not a MVP feature meaning it is
not critical for the Launch version of the App. It will be a add on
once we have proved a demand." MiniMoney launches with the core
task-assignment, budget, Mbucks/Mpoints, and payslip/invoice mechanic
only. Educational curriculum content is a planned **post-launch, v2
feature**, contingent on demonstrated demand for the core product.

**This is a scope decision, not merely an authorship decision, and the
Incubator should trace it as a correction (per Playbook Entry 2), not an
addition, across every section that currently describes curriculum as
in-scope, in-progress, or launch-parallel:**
- **Roadmap**: the curriculum workstream is no longer "starting
  immediately, in parallel with the build" — it moves to a post-launch,
  demand-gated phase.
- **Value Proposition, Problem, Executive Summary**: currently frame
  education as a core, present-tense part of the product's value — these
  need to reflect that the launch product is the financial mechanic
  alone, with education as a stated future direction, not a current
  feature. Do not silently drop MiniMoney's identity as a financial-
  *education* app in the broader sense (the original case study's stated
  purpose) — frame this precisely as an MVP-sequencing decision, not an
  abandonment of the education premise.
- **Success Criteria**: the curriculum-engagement benchmark (30%) becomes
  moot/deferred alongside the feature itself — it should not be scored
  as an open gap for something not being built yet. Reclassify or remove
  it from the near-term success-criteria set, noting it will be
  reintroduced when the curriculum feature is actually built.
- **Curriculum Design (domain extension)**: reframe from "workstream
  committed and starting now, authorship open" to "deferred to v2,
  post-demand-validation; specification work may continue at low
  priority but is not launch-blocking."
- Authorship itself (who eventually writes the curriculum) remains
  genuinely undecided, but is no longer time-pressured, since it is not
  gating anything before launch.
