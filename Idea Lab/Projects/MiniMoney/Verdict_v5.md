# Investment Committee Verdict — MiniMoney v20

## Verdict: Sufficient context — Proceed with changes

The case is not insufficient — the legal foundation (Legal & Compliance,
Child Data & Consent) is genuinely specialist-reviewed and sound, the
product design is coherent, and the document is unusually candid about
its own remaining gaps rather than concealing them. It is also not
disqualified — none of the open items are fundamental flaws in the
business logic itself. But several items reported this cycle as
"resolved" are, on inspection, decisions to proceed without closing the
underlying uncertainty rather than genuine resolutions, and at least one
of these (see Gate Integrity Check below) directly undermines the
document's claim to clear its own completion gate. Proceed, conditional
on the required changes below being addressed before the next stage.

### Required changes

1. **Cost or bound the self-fund-further contingency.** State a maximum
   personal-capital figure or a maximum extension period. As currently
   written (Constraints, Financial Considerations, Critical Gap #1) it is
   directional and unbounded, which the Business Case's own quoted
   Devil's Advocate objection (Self-Certification section) already
   characterizes as "a decision in name only... the absence of one." This
   must be resolved with an actual number, or Constraints' contribution
   to the Readiness Score must be revisited (see Gate Integrity Check).

2. **Make specialist review of the data-breach/incident-response
   commitment a hard pre-pilot gate, not an advisable step.** Currently
   flagged as "advisable... though not required to close this Investment
   Committee item" (Constraints, Roadmap, Child Data & Consent extension).
   Real children's personal data will be collected during the pilot; an
   unreviewed, founder-authored breach protocol should not be in force
   when that happens.

3. **Make specialist review of the exam-bonus motivation-probe a hard
   pre-enrollment gate, not a recommended next step.** This instrument
   (Success Criteria) is the one designed to detect the case's own
   named, accepted residual risk (intrinsic-motivation crowding-out). An
   unreviewed, non-clinician-drafted instrument measuring it undermines
   the purpose of having it.

4. **Decide curriculum authorship, or set a hard decision deadline.**
   Currently explicitly open (Curriculum Design domain extension,
   Roadmap, Outstanding Questions) while the workstream is simultaneously
   described as starting immediately, in parallel with an already-tight
   6-month runway. An unowned "who" on a "starting now" workstream is a
   scheduling risk, not a settled plan.

5. **Lightly scope the schools-partnership channel before treating
   organic-only as the sole, final acquisition strategy.** Market &
   Competition's own research shows the closest local analog (MoneyTime
   SA) reached scale through schools, not organic discovery, and no
   organic-only South African comparable exists anywhere in the case.
   Locking the strategy before even exploratory scoping of the one
   channel with local precedent is a sequencing risk, achievable to
   address at near-zero cost.

6. **Reconcile or bound the child-to-family population-conversion gap**
   underlying the 180-1,830 paying-family revenue range (Market &
   Competition, named residual gap; Revenue & Costs; Financial
   Considerations) — this figure carries every revenue statement in the
   document and currently rests on an applied convention, not a
   derivation.

## Mandatory Gate Integrity Check

**On the two literal self-certified items (expert roster rationale,
Devil's Advocate objections):** self-certification holds up under review
for these two specific bullets. The Business Case reproduces a concrete,
specific excerpt from each (Self-Certification section) — the roster
excerpt names a specific, traceable case assumption (the undocumented
scope of the zero-marginal-cost advice policy), and the Devil's Advocate
excerpt makes a sharp, specific, falsifiable critique (the self-fund
contingency being "the absence of" a constraint) rather than a generic
domain concern. Neither reads as filler produced to check a box.

**However, a distinct and more consequential gate-integrity problem
exists that is not captured by the roster/DA bullets themselves: the
Incubator's own Devil's Advocate objection was surfaced but not acted
upon in the document's own scoring.** The Business Case quotes its
Devil's Advocate calling the self-fund-further contingency a "decision in
name only," yet Constraints is still scored Complete (contributing 5 of
the 91 points) and the Readiness Score section states the case clears
its 70% threshold "with no margin." Applying the Readiness Score
section's own stated standard — "documentation of an open decision is
not resolution of it" — consistently to this item (rather than only to
the motivation-probe and data-breach items, which the document does
correctly keep at Partial/Assumed-tier) would drop Constraints to
Partial, moving the score to 88/130 (67.7%), below the stated 70%
completion-gate threshold. The self-certification section does not
address this internal inconsistency at all — this is the substantive
gap. Producing a genuine, sharp Devil's Advocate objection and then not
reconciling it against the section it directly undermines is a form of
technically-present-but-not-substantively-acted-upon gate integrity, even
though the DA output itself was real work, not filler.

**Recommendation to the Developing Committee / next Incubator cycle:**
either (a) cost the self-fund-further contingency so it is no longer
"documentation of an open decision," restoring the basis for Constraints
= Complete, or (b) accept Constraints = Partial and recompute the
Readiness Score accordingly, and confirm whether the case still clears
70% under that recomputation before treating the completion gate as
passed.

## Risks and Assumptions

*Everything below must be traced by the Developing Committee. Nothing
here may be silently dropped.*

**Financial / execution:**
- Self-fund-further contingency is directional and uncosted — no
  maximum amount or duration exists anywhere in the case.
- R10,000 budget is only coherent given full founder unpaid labor plus
  AI-assisted development; no buffer exists to purchase external
  engineering capacity if founder capacity fails.
- Curriculum content-production cost is entirely unpriced; post-runway
  funding-ask size and form do not exist.
- The 6-month runway must simultaneously contain build, curriculum
  authoring (author undecided), an instrumented pilot, and two
  personally-owned regulatory rechecks — substantially by one person.
- The user's zero-marginal-cost professional-advice policy has
  undocumented scope/limits, repeatedly flagged across sections
  (Financial Considerations, Constraints, Curriculum Design, Outstanding
  Questions) — it is unconfirmed whether it covers instructional design
  or data-privacy practitioner time specifically.

**Regulatory / legal:**
- SARB/National Payment System Act question (account-linking) remains
  externally unsettled; owned by the user at build-spec stage, fallback
  decided (launch without account-linking).
- FPB classification question (Mpoints) remains externally unsettled;
  same ownership/fallback structure (launch without Mpoints); fallback
  removes the 6-9 tier's immediate-feedback loop, a real product-quality
  cost, not a neutral contingency.
- Terminology-risk mitigation (parent-facing-only debt language) has no
  ARB ruling on this fact pattern anywhere — MiniMoney would be a
  first-mover test case if challenged.
- POPIA s14 retention period and deletion trigger remain undesigned.
- Consent-flow documentation separation and lightweight parent
  identity-verification remain unbuilt hardening recommendations.
- Reliance on a single retained lawyer's opinion with no second opinion
  sought.

**Child welfare / data:**
- Data-breach/incident-response commitment is founder-authored and
  explicitly unreviewed by the Data-Privacy Practitioner it names.
- Exam-bonus motivation-probe is Incubator-drafted and explicitly
  unreviewed by the child-development specialist it names.
- The exam-bonus hybrid's residual intrinsic-motivation crowding-out
  risk remains accepted, not resolved, pending that review.
- The late-penalty mechanic's family-stress affective pathway is
  narrowed, not eliminated, per the adopted measurement package.
- A single 20-50-family pilot is being relied upon simultaneously for
  demand signal, mechanic safety, family-stress early warning, and
  motivation-crowding-out signal — four distinct validation burdens on
  one underpowered instrument.

**Market / demand:**
- The n=10 first-party interview finding remains explicitly
  non-representative per standing instruction; not to be treated as a
  validated demand signal.
- Year-1 adoption capture (0.3-1%) and freemium-to-paid conversion
  (1-3%) are both the user's own labeled lowest-confidence, Guessing-tier
  estimates, inferred from non-South-African markets.
- Organic-only acquisition against an 18,000-61,000 family-install
  funnel has no validation plan anywhere in the case and no South
  African comparable for an organic-only approach in this category.
- The schools-partnership channel — the only channel with a demonstrated
  local reach precedent (MoneyTime SA) — has no timeline, target, or
  resourcing plan.
- The child-to-family population-conversion figure underlying the entire
  revenue range (180-1,830 paying families) is an applied convention,
  not a derivation; the average children-per-family distribution is
  unknown.
- MoneyAfrica Kids' premium price is unpublished/unknown; no
  South-Africa-specific comparable exists for FamZoo/Bomad-style
  products.

**Product / curriculum:**
- Curriculum content does not exist; authorship is explicitly undecided
  between the founder and an as-yet-unconfirmed advice-policy route.
- Success-criteria benchmarks (curriculum engagement 30%, operational
  health 65%) carry no external benchmark; retention has no proposed
  figure at all.
- Three-tier curriculum age framework is not yet reconciled with the
  product's six-way stakeholder sub-bands.
- Dispute-escalation beyond the 48-hour window has no formal resolution
  mechanism.

**Gate-integrity / process:**
- The internal inconsistency between the document's own Devil's Advocate
  objection (self-fund contingency as "the absence of" a constraint) and
  Constraints' Complete status/contribution to the 70% Readiness Score
  is unresolved and must be traced (see Gate Integrity Check above).
- Two Investment Committee required changes from the prior cycle were
  closed by Incubator-drafted candidate content rather than by obtaining
  the specialist expertise the underlying gap called for; both remain
  Evidence: Assumed.
