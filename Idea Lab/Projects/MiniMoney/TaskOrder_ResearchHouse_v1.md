## Case
MiniMoney — financial-education app for children aged 6-18, South Africa,
Android-first, Freemium model.

## What is being asked
For each numbered item below, provide:
1. **Where** — what it would change or resolve in the Business Case (which
   gap it closes).
2. **When** — a realistic estimate of how long the research/verification
   would take to complete (e.g. hours of desk research vs. requires a
   commissioned survey vs. requires a paid legal opinion — flag which
   category each item falls into).
3. **By how much** — your honest estimate of how much this would improve
   the item's evidence quality (e.g. moves a figure from [Guessing]/
   Assumed to [Likely]/Supported, or cannot realistically be resolved by
   desk research at all and requires a professional/legal engagement
   outside your scope).

Where an item requires a paid, professional, or legal service Research
House cannot itself perform (e.g. an actual POPIA/ARB legal opinion, a
commissioned parent survey, a paid market-research report), say so
plainly and estimate only the *scoping* value you can add (e.g. finding
the right firm/survey vendor, identifying likely cost ranges) rather than
attempting the underlying professional judgment yourself.

## Flagged items

1. **South Africa-specific kids'-app / fintech-app adoption and
   install-conversion benchmarks.** The current funnel model (6.1M
   reachable households, 0.3-1% Year-1 install capture) is extrapolated
   from UK/US comparables (GoHenry, Greenlight) with no South African
   data point. This is flagged as the single weakest link in the entire
   quantitative case.

2. **Local pricing benchmark for a comparable South African product.**
   No competitor (African Bank MyWORLD Power Pocket, MoneyAfrica Kids,
   MoneyTime SA) has a confirmed public price point to validate
   willingness-to-pay against.

3. **South Africa-specific contextual mobile ad eCPM data**, if an
   ads-supported hybrid Freemium tier is pursued (see item 15 below) —
   currently only a general global pattern is cited, no SA figure.

4. **Whether the ads-hybrid (2-4%) or subscription-only (1-3%)
   Freemium conversion benchmark is the one to validate against** — this
   depends on a monetization design decision not yet made (see item 15).

5. **Any existing published demand signal** for kids'-financial-education
   apps in South Africa specifically (app store review counts/ratings for
   MoneyAfrica Kids, any public usage data for MoneyTime SA, any press
   coverage of African Bank's MyWORLD Power Pocket adoption).

6. **App-store child-category policy specifics** (Apple App Store and
   Google Play) regarding a cosmetic, non-monetary rewards store
   (MiniMoney's "Mpoints" store) aimed at children under 13 — does either
   platform's kids'-category policy impose disclosure or design
   requirements even absent real-money transactions?

7. **Data retention/deletion policy norms** for child-directed apps
   operating under POPIA specifically (not GDPR-K or COPPA) — what do
   comparable South African child-directed digital products typically
   commit to?

8. **Whether "payslip," "invoice," "late penalty," and "arrears"
   terminology carries any documented regulatory or consumer-protection
   precedent in South Africa** when applied to a minor — i.e. has this
   specific terminology-to-minors question been addressed in any prior
   ARB ruling, National Credit Regulator guidance, or comparable
   consumer-protection context?

9. **Cost-range scoping (not a quote) for a POPIA/ARB-specialist legal
   opinion** in South Africa — typical engagement scope and cost range
   for confirming (a) the universal parental-consent model's sufficiency
   and (b) the ad-targeting-to-minors restrictions, if pursued.

10. **General industry cost-range scoping** for the specific technical
    build described (budget-setting flow, dual-currency ledger, task
    engine, exam-bonus calculator, photo-proof capture, dispute/payment
    workflows, document generation) — refine the existing "tens of
    thousands to low hundreds of thousands of USD-equivalent" range with
    any more specific South African/offshore development-cost benchmarks
    if available.
