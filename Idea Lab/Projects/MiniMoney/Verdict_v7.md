# Investment Committee Verdict: MiniMoney (v23)

> Based on `ExecutiveSummary.md` and `BusinessCase_v23.md` only. No other
> case-folder file was read. This verdict is independent of, and was
> formed without access to, any prior Investment Committee cycle
> referenced inside the Business Case.

## Verdict

**Sufficient context — Proceed with changes.**

The case has abundant, honestly-tiered information across a mature
23-version document; this is not a case of missing context. The legal
and regulatory foundation is genuinely solid (specific case-law citations,
a low-risk money-transmission structure contingent on a locked
product-spec constraint, both open regulatory questions carrying a named
owner and a decided launch fallback). However, several items currently
scored as non-blocking are, in this Committee's assessment, load-bearing
enough to require resolution — or at minimum a committed near-term plan —
before this case proceeds further. The required changes below are
proportionate to the strength already demonstrated: they do not ask for
new research engagements, only for completion of work that is already
scoped, already zero-cost in most cases, and already named as owed by the
Business Case itself.

### Required Changes

1. **Complete both trigger-defined specialist reviews** — the
   Data-Privacy Practitioner's review of the candidate data-breach/
   incident-response commitment, and the child-development specialist's
   review of the candidate exam-bonus motivation-probe — before any real
   family is enrolled in the pilot. Both are stated as zero marginal cost
   and have not occurred as of this revision despite the document's
   references to at least six prior Investment Committee cycles. Evidence
   that the review actually occurred, not merely that its trigger is
   defined, is required before this case is considered pilot-ready.
2. **Produce a minimal P&L / break-even model** — fixed costs (R10,000
   development budget; R20,000 self-fund ceiling) against per-family
   contribution margin at R59.99/month. This is a bounded,
   founder-executable arithmetic task, not a research engagement, and has
   been the sole named reason Financial Considerations remains Partial
   across multiple stated cycles.
3. **Confirm the two founder-specific facts gating the named funding
   fallback** — the founder's age (gates NYDA's 18-35 eligibility band,
   the best-timelined candidate) and MiniMoney's company-registration/
   SARS tax-clearance status (gates the SEDA Technology Programme). Both
   are answerable facts about the founder and the business, not open
   research questions.
4. **Define a quantified stop/redesign threshold for the child-welfare
   instruments** — the family-relationship-strain measurement package and
   the exam-bonus motivation-probe currently have no equivalent to the
   commercial pilot's 60% acquisition-checkpoint kill-metric. State what
   result on these instruments would halt or force redesign of the pilot.
5. **Provide a concrete, named organic-acquisition channel plan**
   underlying the 0.3-1% Year-1 install-capture assumption — currently
   sourced only from adjacent international markets (GoHenry/Greenlight
   UK/US) with no South Africa-specific, bottom-up channel rationale. At
   minimum, name which organic channels the founder intends to use.
6. **Provide an aggregate founder-capacity/bandwidth assessment** across
   all concurrent pre-launch obligations owed by a single solopreneur
   within the 6-month runway: the build, the instrumented pilot, two
   regulatory rechecks, two specialist reviews, and manual dispute
   adjudication beyond 48 hours. Each is currently assessed individually;
   none assesses the aggregate single-point-of-failure risk this creates.

None of these six changes require a new Research House engagement — all
are either founder-executable directly (P&L, founder facts, channel plan,
capacity assessment) or are already-scoped, already-budgeted specialist
work that has simply not yet been performed (the two reviews).

## Mandatory Gate Integrity Check

This Committee did not read `ExpertRoster.md` or `reviews/DevilsAdvocate.md`
directly — by design, per this Committee's isolation mandate — and
therefore cannot assess their literal content. This check is based
entirely on whether the substantive content of `BusinessCase_v23.md` is
consistent with those self-certified items having done genuine,
pressure-tested work.

**Self-certification does not fully hold up under review. One specific
pattern appears thin: the repeated treatment of "trigger defined" as
equivalent to "resolved."**

The Business Case's own Self-Certification section reports the Devil's
Advocate was "regenerated this cycle, five objections," each "citing a
specific section" — technically present. But the substantive pattern
visible across the Business Case itself raises doubt about how hard that
internal Devil's Advocate function pressed on one specific, recurring
issue: six of seven required changes carried forward from a prior
Investment Committee cycle were resolved by defining a trigger, naming an
owner, or naming a fallback category — not by completing the underlying
work. Two specialist reviews, both stated as zero marginal cost, remain
unperformed after at least six referenced Investment Committee cycles,
each time restated as "trigger-defined, not yet satisfied" without
apparent challenge to why the trigger condition ("once a stable working
model exists") has itself never been met across that many cycles. If an
internal Devil's Advocate process genuinely pressure-tested this pattern
each cycle, it is not evident from the Business Case's text that doing so
changed anything about the pattern's recurrence — the same structural
move (define-trigger-in-lieu-of-resolve) reappears at least twice more in
this very cycle (the funding-target "resolution," which names a candidate
list without confirming obtainability, and the continued absence of a
P&L despite the gap being named as the sole blocker to Financial
Considerations across multiple cycles).

This is not a claim that the Devil's Advocate document doesn't exist or
that it lacks the required count of objections — this Committee has no
way to verify that either way without breaching its own isolation
mandate, and does not attempt to. It is a claim, grounded entirely in
`BusinessCase_v23.md`'s own visible content, that whatever objections
were raised did not durably interrupt this specific recurring pattern.
Required Change 1 above (complete, don't merely re-trigger, the two
outstanding specialist reviews) is the direct, actionable response to
this concern.

Beyond this one item, self-certification appears to hold up: every
section is tagged Status/Confidence/Evidence as claimed; no
Critical-tagged section is scored Incomplete; the Readiness Score
arithmetic (94/130 = 72.3%, or 91/130 = 70.0% conservative) is
internally consistent with the section-by-section table as presented.

## Risks and Assumptions

The following are flagged for the Developing Committee to trace against.
Nothing below may be silently dropped downstream.

**Regulatory / Legal**
- SARB/NPS Act open-banking treatment of optional account-linking:
  unresolved, owned by the user, fallback is launch-without-linking, no
  stated deadline within the 6-month runway.
- FPB classification of Mpoints: unresolved, owned by the user, fallback
  is launch-without-Mpoints (which itself thins subscription appeal), no
  ARB ruling directly on point — a genuine interpretive gap, not a
  settled one.
- Mbucks non-transferability is a locked, binding product-spec constraint
  — any future roadmap change requires the entire money-transmitter
  analysis to be redone.
- POPIA Section 14 retention period and deletion trigger: not yet
  affirmatively designed — a named, pending build task.
- Two hardening recommendations (consent-flow documentation separation;
  lightweight parent identity-verification) remain unbuilt.
- Terminology-risk mitigation (debt-coded language reserved to
  parent-facing surfaces) rests on reasoned judgment, not settled ARB
  authority.

**Child Welfare / Data Privacy**
- Data-Privacy Practitioner review of the candidate breach-response
  commitment: not yet performed, zero-cost, trigger-defined but
  outstanding across multiple cycles. Real children's data must not be
  collected until this occurs — a discipline the document commits to but
  has not yet exercised.
- Child-development specialist review of the exam-bonus motivation-probe:
  not yet performed, same trigger, same outstanding status. The
  underlying crowding-out risk is described as "confirmed, not resolved."
- No quantified stop/redesign threshold exists for either the
  relationship-strain or motivation-probe instruments, unlike the
  commercial acquisition checkpoint.
- Late-penalty mechanic's Family Stress Model affective pathway is
  "narrowed but not eliminated" — residual real-world household-conflict
  risk to minors as young as 6.

**Financial**
- No P&L or break-even analysis exists anywhere in this Business Case —
  a distinct, fully open material question untouched across multiple
  cycles.
- Revenue funnel (92-915 paying families) rests on Guessing/Assumed-tier
  assumptions: 2.0 children per family (Assumed, not derived); 0.3-1%
  install capture and 1-3% conversion (both Guessing, sourced from
  adjacent international markets, not South Africa-specific).
- R10,000 development budget is 45×-220× below a previously cited agency
  engineering cost comparable — viable only if founder-labor-plus-AI
  substitution genuinely holds.
- Self-fund-further ceiling (R20,000 total) and its named non-dilutive
  funding fallback are both untested for sufficiency/obtainability.
- Founder's age and MiniMoney's company-registration/tax-clearance status
  — both unconfirmed, both gate the top two named funding-fallback
  candidates (NYDA, SEDA).
- No precise sizing exists for the post-runway external funding ask;
  researched figures are program ceilings, not typical disbursements.

**Commercial / Go-to-Market**
- The organic-only acquisition thesis (reaching 9,150-30,500 Year-1
  family installs with zero paid acquisition and zero marketing budget)
  has no validation mechanism in the current plan — the pilot's 60%
  checkpoint tests engagement, not acquisition-volume feasibility, a
  distinction the Business Case itself concedes explicitly.
- The schools-partnership channel — the strongest demonstrated local
  reach precedent in the category — is deferred entirely for
  founder-bandwidth reasons.
- n=10 first-party interview evidence is genuine but explicitly
  non-representative; must not be treated as a demand signal until
  superseded by the pilot or a structured survey.
- Success-criteria benchmarks (operational health 65%, general retention)
  remain unbenchmarked placeholders.

**Execution Capacity**
- A single founder is responsible, within one 6-month runway, for: the
  build; an instrumented pilot; two regulatory rechecks; two specialist
  reviews; and manual dispute adjudication beyond 48 hours (explicitly
  named as non-scalable). No section assesses this as an aggregate
  single-point-of-failure risk.
- The dispute-escalation interim mechanism has no stated volume or
  capacity threshold at which it is deemed to have failed.

**Process / Framework**
- A recurring pattern across at least six referenced Investment Committee
  cycles converts "unresolved" into "resolved via defined trigger, owner,
  and fallback" without completing the underlying work — flagged above
  under Gate Integrity Check as the one item warranting scrutiny. The
  Developing Committee should trace whether the two outstanding
  specialist reviews are actually completed, not merely re-triggered
  again, before any pilot-stage work proceeds.
