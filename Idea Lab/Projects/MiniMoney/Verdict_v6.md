# Investment Committee — Verdict: MiniMoney v21

## Verdict

**Sufficient context — Proceed with changes.**

**The case:** `BusinessCase_v21.md` presents a well-grounded, self-aware
document with unusually strong legal foundations for its stage (retained
specialist legal opinion covering all seven originally-scoped questions
at Verified tier, a resolved POPIA consent model, verified minor-
contract-capacity case law, and named owners/fallbacks for both
externally-unsettled regulatory questions). It also demonstrates real
process integrity: it discloses its own prior arithmetic error in the
revenue funnel and corrects it downward rather than leaving the more
favorable figure standing, and it discloses its own prior scoring
failure (v20's asserted 70.0% readiness score would have honestly been
67.7% under the document's own standard) rather than obscuring it. This
is not a case built on thin or evasive documentation — it is detailed,
internally cross-referenced, and largely honest about its own limits.

Against that, no disqualifying legal, ethical, or structural blocker
exists: money-transmitter risk is assessed low and contingent on a
locked, enforceable product-spec constraint; minor contractual capacity
is the case's strongest legal position; both remaining regulatory
questions (SARB/NPS Act, FPB) carry named owners and decided,
launch-viable fallbacks rather than open-ended exposure. This case
should proceed, not stop — but not yet as currently specified.

What keeps this from a clean "Proceed": two safety-critical specialist
reviews (data-breach/incident-response commitment; exam-bonus
motivation-probe) are correctly identified as hard gates but have **not
yet occurred**, and nothing in the document evidences they are actually
scheduled; the revenue funnel and acquisition strategy rest entirely on
Guessing/Assumed-tier assumptions with no validation plan for the
central open question (can organic-only acquisition reach the funnel at
all); the R20,000 total self-fund ceiling's sufficiency is untested; and
the Readiness Score itself clears its own 70% gate by a thin, self-
disclosed margin (72.3%, or exactly 70.0% with no margin under a
disclosed conservative reading) on the back of a status change the
Incubator itself flags as a judgment call rather than an unambiguous
resolution.

### Required Changes

1. **Do not treat "hard gate created" as equivalent to "gate cleared."**
   Before pilot enrollment begins, obtain documented confirmation that
   (a) the Data-Privacy Practitioner has actually reviewed the
   data-breach/incident-response commitment, and (b) the child-
   development specialist has actually reviewed the motivation-probe
   instrument — both with dated sign-off, not merely a restated
   commitment that the review "must" occur.
2. **Confirm the zero-marginal-cost advice policy actually covers both
   specialist reviews** named above, given the policy's scope is
   currently undocumented (Assumptions, Revenue & Costs).
3. **Define the beyond-48-hour dispute-escalation mechanism** before
   pilot enrollment, not post-launch — the pilot itself will generate
   real parent-child payment disputes.
4. **Stress-test viability at the low end of the revenue range** (92
   paying families, ~R5,519/month), not primarily the upper end, and
   state explicitly what happens to the runway and the "proved demand"
   v2-curriculum trigger if actual results land there.
5. **Define an explicit acquisition checkpoint/kill-metric** for the
   transition from pilot to wider release, given organic-only
   acquisition currently has no validation plan anywhere in the case.
6. **Quantify the v2-curriculum "proved demand" trigger** (a specific
   subscriber count, retention figure, or pilot outcome) before the
   pilot concludes, so the trigger can actually be evaluated against
   results rather than remaining an undefined phrase.
7. **Identify at least one concrete non-dilutive funding target with a
   realistic timeline** as the named fallback for the R20,000 self-fund
   ceiling, given the runway itself is the stated binding constraint and
   grant/program timelines are not accounted for.

None of these changes require re-opening the legal, consent, or
contract-capacity analysis, which this Committee finds sound. They are
concentrated on converting process commitments (gates, checkpoints,
targets) that currently exist only as stated intentions into confirmed,
scheduled, or quantified items before the pilot — the phase where real
children's data and real families' money are first at stake.

## Mandatory Gate Integrity Check

This Committee's isolation mandate prohibits reading `ExpertRoster.md`
or `reviews/DevilsAdvocate.md` directly — only `BusinessCase_v21.md`'s
own narration of their contents was available. **This limits the scope
of this check to what is visible through the Business Case's
self-certification section and its surrounding argumentation; it is not
an independent audit of those two files' actual content.**

Within that scope: **self-certification does not appear superficial on
the evidence available.** Specifically:

- The Business Case volunteers, rather than conceals, its own prior
  scoring failure — it states plainly that v20's asserted 70.0% score
  would have been 67.7% under the document's own "Complete" standard
  had that standard been applied consistently to the self-fund-further
  item (Readiness Score section). Concealing this would have been easy;
  it was not concealed.
- The one status change beyond directly-required fixes this cycle
  (Curriculum Design: Partial → Complete) is explicitly labeled "a
  judgment call, disclosed explicitly for Investment Committee
  scrutiny, not asserted flatly," with a conservative alternative score
  shown alongside it (Readiness Score). This is the opposite of
  asserting a superficial pass silently.
- A traced ripple-effect check is demonstrated in substance, not just
  claimed: the curriculum-deferral decision is followed through to
  Fintech Advance (a feature not itself named in the original
  clarification but sharing the "curriculum" category) across thirteen
  named sections (What Changed in v21, item 4).

**One item flagged for continued scrutiny, though it is disclosed
rather than hidden and therefore does not itself constitute a
superficial self-certification failure:** the "hard gate" elevation for
the two specialist reviews changes the *requirement's bindingness*, not
its *completion status* — the Business Case says this explicitly
(Stakeholders section: "the gate elevation changes the requirement's
bindingness, not its completion status"). Because this is stated
plainly rather than obscured, it does not fail the Gate Integrity Check
on its own terms — but this Committee flags it as the item most likely
to be mistaken for risk-closure by a reader skimming status labels
rather than the underlying text, and requires it be tracked forward
(see Required Changes 1-2).

**Limitation on this check, stated explicitly:** because this Committee
did not read `ExpertRoster.md` or `reviews/DevilsAdvocate.md` directly,
it cannot certify that those files' actual sentence counts, specificity,
or objection quality meet the Business Case's self-reported claims (six
roster entries, five Devil's Advocate objections, each citing specific
case content). That verification, if desired, falls outside this
Committee's permitted scope by design and would need to occur elsewhere
in the framework.

## Risks and Assumptions

Everything below is flagged for the Developing Committee to trace
forward; nothing here should be silently dropped downstream.

1. Two hard-gate specialist reviews (data-breach commitment;
   motivation-probe) are process-required but **not yet completed** —
   must be confirmed with dated sign-off before pilot enrollment.
2. Whether the zero-marginal-cost advice policy actually covers both
   specialists' time is undocumented/Assumed.
3. SARB/NPS Act open-banking question remains externally unresolved
   (owner: user; fallback: launch without account-linking) — timeline
   unspecified.
4. FPB classification of Mpoints remains externally unresolved (owner:
   user; fallback: launch without Mpoints), with traced ripple effects
   on the cosmetic store, the 6-9 age tier's immediate-feedback loop,
   and Google Play's loyalty-point disclosure requirement.
5. Entire revenue funnel (adoption 0.3-1%, conversion 1-3%, 2.0
   children/family) is Guessing/Assumed-tier, inferred from non-South
   African comparables, unvalidated locally.
6. No validation plan exists anywhere in the case for whether
   organic-only acquisition can reach the 9,150-30,500 install funnel.
7. Schools-partnership channel — the only channel with demonstrated
   local reach precedent (MoneyTime SA) — is explicitly deferred for
   founder-bandwidth reasons; this relocates, not reduces, acquisition
   risk.
8. v2-curriculum "proved demand" trigger is unquantified — risk of
   indefinite deferral or premature investment on an ambiguous signal.
9. Curriculum authorship (founder vs. engaged designer) remains
   genuinely undecided.
10. Execution-capacity risk: solo founder must complete build (~3
    months), instrumented 20-50 family pilot, two regulatory rechecks,
    and two specialist reviews inside a 6-month runway with minimal
    financial slack (R20,000 total ceiling described in the case's own
    words as buying "days, not months" of professional engineering if
    founder capacity fails).
11. R20,000 total self-fund ceiling's sufficiency to reach
    sustainability is untested; grant/startup-program fallback's actual
    availability is unconfirmed.
12. POPIA s14 retention period and deletion trigger not yet
    affirmatively designed; consent-flow documentation separation and
    lightweight parent identity-verification remain unbuilt.
13. Dispute-escalation mechanism beyond the 48-hour window is
    undefined — a live gap given the pilot will generate real disputes.
14. Exam-bonus hybrid's residual intrinsic-motivation risk is accepted,
    not eliminated; pilot (20-50 families) is explicitly underpowered
    for the borrowed validated instruments — findings will be
    qualitative/directional only.
15. Family-relationship-strain risk from the late-penalty mechanic:
    mitigations adopted, but residual risk stated as "real" in the
    document itself.
16. n=10 first-party interview data must not be treated as a
    representative demand signal (standing instruction reaffirmed this
    cycle).
17. Curriculum Design section's "Complete" status is a self-disclosed
    judgment call; the conservative reading (70.0%, no margin) should
    not be forgotten in favor of the primary reading (72.3%).
18. No P&L or break-even analysis exists; no external funding-ask size
    or form is sized beyond the R20,000 self-fund ceiling.
19. No acquisition checkpoint/kill-metric defined for the transition
    from pilot to wider release.
20. ARB Code terminology risk: mitigation adopted, but no ARB ruling
    directly addresses this fact pattern — a genuine interpretive gap.
21. This Committee's Gate Integrity Check was necessarily scoped to
    what the Business Case narrates about `ExpertRoster.md` and
    `reviews/DevilsAdvocate.md`, not those files directly — any future
    review with broader access should independently verify those
    artifacts' substantive content.
