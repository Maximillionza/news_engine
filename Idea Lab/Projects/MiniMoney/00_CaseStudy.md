## Raw idea as presented (verbatim from user)
i want  you to assess my idea for a Financial App called Mini Money. The purpose of the app is to educate young children from the age of 6 to 18 on different aspects of financial education

I want to allow kids to earn points equivilent to real money through the assigning of tasks, cover expenses which are % based and therefor scalable regardless of earnings. This is predetermined by the app but adjustable by the parent. The parent is automatically issued an invoice with the respecitve earnings and the kid recieves a 'payslip' outlining their earnings, 'overtime', expenses ect. The parent is then required to make the payment via thier registered bank to the kid for the expected value.

Education needs to be incorporated and designed to appeal to the respective age demographic.

Reasoning:
The submission specifies a clear target demographic (ages 6-18), a defined core mechanic (task-based point earning mapped to real currency, percentage-based scalable "expenses," parent-adjustable rules, automated invoice/payslip generation, and a real bank payment obligation triggered by the app), and an explicit design constraint (age-appropriate financial education layered on top of the mechanic). That is enough substance for the Incubator to identify the relevant domain (fintech + edtech + family/parental-control apps), build an expert roster (financial literacy curriculum design, child-directed UX, payments/banking compliance, parental consent frameworks), and run gap analysis. Open questions — regulatory status of a minor-directed app that triggers real bank transfers, KYC/compliance obligations, specific age-band curriculum splits, platform choice, monetization model — are exactly the kind of gaps the Incubator is designed to surface and score, not a reason to reject at intake.
