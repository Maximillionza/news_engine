## Case
MiniMoney — financial-education app for children aged 6-18, South
Africa, Android-first, subscription-based (R59.99/month, up to 4
children). Solopreneur venture with a R10,000 initial development budget
and a hard R20,000 total self-fund ceiling (R10,000 initial + R10,000
additional cap) before external funding becomes necessary. Professional
opinions (legal, specialist reviews) are obtained at zero marginal cost
via the founder's own existing advice policy — this does not extend to
general startup funding.

## What is being asked
Identify concrete, realistic **non-dilutive funding options** (grants,
startup programs, accelerators, competitions) available to a South
African solo-founder, pre-revenue-proven, fintech-adjacent/edtech
mobile-app venture — specifically as a fallback if the R20,000 self-fund
ceiling is reached without the venture being self-sustaining.

Specifically:

1. **Named South African (or accessible-to-South-African-founders)
   grant or non-dilutive funding programs** relevant to an early-stage,
   solo-founder, fintech-adjacent or edtech mobile app — e.g. innovation
   grants, sector-specific accelerators (fintech, edtech, youth-focused),
   government or quasi-government innovation funds (e.g. any relevant
   NEF, SEDA, or Department of Science and Innovation-linked programs,
   if applicable), or corporate/foundation-backed startup programs.

2. **Realistic timelines** for each identified option — application
   windows, typical time-to-decision, and typical time-to-disbursement.
   This matters specifically because the case's 6-month runway is tight;
   a program with a 9-month application-to-disbursement cycle is not a
   realistic fallback for a founder who has just exhausted a self-fund
   ceiling.

3. **Eligibility fit** — flag plainly where a program likely excludes
   this venture (e.g. requires existing revenue, requires a team of
   more than one, requires a different sector focus, requires South
   African company registration in a form not yet confirmed) rather than
   listing it without qualification.

4. **Rough typical award size**, where publicly stated, so the founder
   can judge whether a given program is even proportionate to the
   R20,000+ gap being addressed.

## What is explicitly NOT being asked
- Do not recommend equity/dilutive investment options — the brief is
  specifically for non-dilutive funding, per the founder's stated
  preference.
- Do not fabricate a program or a deadline if you cannot confirm one
  publicly exists or is currently active — say so plainly, and note
  where a program's current-cycle status would need direct confirmation
  from the program itself.
- Do not attempt to assess whether any specific program would actually
  approve this venture — that is not knowable from desk research; your
  job is to identify realistic candidates and their stated eligibility
  criteria/timelines, not to predict outcomes.
