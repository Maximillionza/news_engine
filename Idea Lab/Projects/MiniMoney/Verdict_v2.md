# Investment Committee Verdict — MiniMoney (v15 cycle)

## Verdict: Insufficient context to assess

This Committee cannot render a Proceed / Proceed with Changes / Do Not
Proceed judgment on `BusinessCase_v15.md` as it currently stands, because
several foundational unknowns are not gaps at the margin of an otherwise
assessable case — they are gaps in the two dimensions that most determine
whether this venture is legally and commercially viable at all. This is
not primarily a criticism of the Incubator's documentation quality, which
this cycle's own text is candid and well-sourced about; it is a statement
that the underlying real-world inputs this Committee would need to weigh
have not yet been obtained by anyone, at any stage.

Specifically missing:

1. **The specialist POPIA/ARB/contract-law legal opinion.** Required
   since v9 (six versions ago), still described in this cycle as
   "still-uncommissioned" (Executive Summary; Legal & Compliance ★).
   Without it, this Committee cannot assess whether the core mechanic —
   a "payslip"/"invoice"/escalating "late penalty" system applied to
   minors, even in a non-custodial structure — is legally sound, requires
   redesign, or is disqualifying. This is a Critical-weighted section by
   the Business Case's own scoring, and remains Partial.

2. **The child-development/age-appropriateness expert review of the
   late-penalty mechanic.** Required since v9, still not commissioned
   (Stakeholders; Risks; Validation Strategy). Without it, this Committee
   cannot assess whether the parent-only, child-invisible redesign is a
   genuine risk mitigation or a relocation of the same risk to an
   unmeasured channel.

3. **Actual curriculum design content.** Age-band splits, instructional
   format, standards alignment, and content authorship are all
   unspecified (Curriculum Design, Domain Extension) — Confidence
   explicitly downgraded to Low this cycle. For a product whose stated
   identity is a "financial-education app," this Committee cannot
   evaluate the core value proposition to children without knowing what
   the education actually is.

4. **Financial-modeling ambiguities that prevent a reliable unit-economics
   read:** the family-vs-child install-count ambiguity (Financial
   Considerations, Critical Gap #2) and the subscriber-count discrepancy
   (180-1,830 computed vs. 360-2,440 cited — Revenue & Costs, Critical
   Gap #5), both unresolved across multiple versions.

5. **The free-vs-subscription feature split** (Value Proposition,
   Outstanding Questions) — without it, this Committee cannot fully
   evaluate what a non-paying family experiences, which bears directly
   on conversion-rate assumptions.

6. **The third named competitor's identity and any structured market
   sizing** (Market & Competition, Outstanding Questions) — the "no
   direct South African incumbent" claim is plausible but not
   independently verifiable from what is available.

This Committee notes, and credits, genuine progress in this cycle and
its predecessors: subscription pricing is Verified-tier resolved
(R59.99/month, up to 4 children); the 90-day-vs-annual funnel tension is
resolved with a user-confirmed floor-plus-ramp model; the
pre-link independent-registration carve-out was removed, closing a real
POPIA exposure; account-linking is now optional rather than a permanent
exclusion. None of this is disputed. But these resolved items sit
alongside unresolved items that are, by the Business Case's own
Critical-weighting scheme, more consequential — and the venture's own
completion gate, applied to this same document, concludes it does not
pass (Readiness Score 52% against a stated 70% threshold; Self-
Certification section, `BusinessCase_v15.md`). This Committee's
independent verdict aligns with, though is not derived from, that
self-assessment.

**What would resolve "insufficient context" into an assessable verdict:**
items 1-3 above obtained (even preliminary versions — a "preliminary
legal read" is already referenced in the case as a lower-cost interim
option and has not been pursued); items 4-6 reconciled or answered. None
of these require another Incubator writing cycle alone — they require
external inputs (a commissioned legal opinion, a commissioned
child-development review, actual curriculum-authoring work) that no
number of further desk-research or clarification cycles can substitute
for.

## Mandatory Gate Integrity Check

This Committee has no access to `ExpertRoster.md` or
`reviews/DevilsAdvocate.md` and did not read them, per its mandated
isolation from the Incubator's internal process artifacts. This check is
therefore based solely on what `BusinessCase_v15.md`'s own
Self-Certification section claims about those items, and on whether the
Business Case's visible content is consistent with substantive (not
merely superficial) underlying work.

**No clear evidence of superficiality was found from what is visible.**
The Business Case text itself displays a pattern consistent with genuine
adversarial self-scrutiny having occurred somewhere in its process:
confidence is actively downgraded where warranted (Curriculum Design,
Medium to Low), limitations are disclosed rather than papered over (the
explicit "disclosed limitation" framing used for both Market &
Competition and Curriculum Design), and the Self-Certification section
itself honestly reports a FAIL on the Readiness Score threshold rather
than asserting a pass. This is circumstantial evidence against
superficial self-certification, though this Committee cannot verify it
directly.

**One structural concern is flagged, not as a finding of superficiality,
but as a gate-design weakness this Committee cannot resolve from its
permitted inputs:** the Self-Certification section's own stated criteria
for the expert roster and Devil's Advocate items are format-based —
"entries ≥3 sentences, each naming a specific case-study assumption" and
"objections ≥3, each citing a specific section" — rather than
substance-based. A roster entry or objection can satisfy a sentence-count
and citation requirement without being probing or well-reasoned. This
Committee has no way to confirm from `BusinessCase_v15.md` or
`ExecutiveSummary.md` alone whether the underlying roster and Devil's
Advocate transcript clear this bar in substance as well as in form — that
determination sits outside this Committee's permitted inputs by design,
and should not be treated as resolved simply because this Committee found
no contrary evidence.

## Risks and Assumptions (for Developing Committee traceability)

1. Specialist legal opinion (POPIA, minor contractual capacity,
   domestic-agreement presumption, ARB/NCR precedent) — required since
   v9, still uncommissioned as of v15.
2. NCR applicability to read-only account-linking — unresolved by desk
   research; currently a reasoned inference, not a regulator statement
   (`ResearchFindings_v3.md` Item 2, as cited in Legal & Compliance).
3. Consent-gate technical implementation (as distinct from the resolved
   policy commitment) — undocumented.
4. Two explicit user risk-accepted assumptions (POPIA Section 14
   retention sufficiency; exam-bonus mechanic's no-schools-data-privacy
   assumption) — accepted by the business owner, unreviewed by any
   specialist.
5. Google Play Families Policy loyalty-point disclosure requirement
   (Mpoints) and Apple Kids Category IAP-currency question — both open.
6. Child-development/age-appropriateness expert review of the late-penalty
   mechanic — required since v9, not commissioned.
7. Curriculum design — age-band splits, instructional format, standards
   alignment, content authorship — all unspecified; Confidence
   downgraded to Low this cycle.
8. Family-vs-child install-count ambiguity — unresolved; directly affects
   revenue and funnel modeling.
9. Subscriber-count discrepancy (180-1,830 computed vs. 360-2,440 cited) —
   unreconciled across multiple versions.
10. Free-vs-subscription feature split — unknown; affects value
    proposition and conversion modeling.
11. Third named competitor's identity — unknown; no structured market
    sizing exists.
12. The n=10 first-party interview data point is explicitly bounded, by
    standing instruction since v9/v10, as non-representative — it must
    not be treated as a validated demand signal anywhere downstream
    (funnel modeling, Financial Considerations, Objectives, or elsewhere)
    until superseded by the planned 20-50 family pilot or a structured
    survey, or its methodology is retroactively documented.
13. Schools-partnership distribution channel — timeline, target school
    count, and resourcing plan are all undefined, despite being treated
    as an intended parallel channel in funnel and opportunity framing.
14. Engineering-cost range ($25,000-$120,000+) is reconciled "in kind,
    not in amount" against the solopreneur/AI-assisted build model —
    actual cost displacement remains open; no budget ceiling exists for
    the "external expertise on-demand" line.
15. The legal-opinion commissioning trigger is event-based ("once a
    stable working model exists, and before any pilot testing with real
    families begins") rather than dated — flagged by the Incubator itself
    across five consecutive revisions without resolution.
16. Readiness Score has been stagnant at 52% across five consecutive
    versions (v11 through v15) — a governance/execution-cadence signal
    independent of any single section's content.
17. The late-penalty mechanic's parent-only, child-invisible redesign
    narrows but does not eliminate child-development risk; the reframed
    indirect-stress success criterion has no adopted, concrete metric.
18. Mbucks-to-Rand peg is fixed; flexibility/inflation-adjustment
    mechanism is unaddressed (Outstanding Questions).
19. Retention has no proposed figure at all (Success Criteria).
20. Curriculum-engagement (30%) and operational-health (65%) success
    criteria have no external benchmark.
21. Residual ambiguity: whether a distinct post-registration,
    pre-budget-link stage still exists within the now fully
    parent-supervised flow (Operations, Legal & Compliance — Child Data &
    Consent ★).
22. iOS porting is deferred to future work; Apple-specific compliance
    (Kids Category) is therefore unresolved for a platform not yet
    scheduled.
23. Advertising monetization is deferred to a possible post-launch V2 —
    unvalidated as a revenue diversification path.
24. The Business Case's own Self-Certification concludes it does not pass
    its own completion gate (Readiness Score 52% vs. a stated 70%
    threshold) — this Committee's verdict is independent of, but
    consistent with, that self-assessment.
